What it means: Every model leaned too wet — none fully anticipated the record dryness, though all were within ~2 mm/day. ICON came closest on the monthly total (47 vs 45 mm); ECMWF and GFS over-predicted by ~35–40%. Notably, NASA POWER — the satellite estimate the live dashboard uses for its "rains active" check — reads wettest (70 mm), so it over-states rainfall here vs CHIRPS. The ground truth was drier than every automated source suggested.
